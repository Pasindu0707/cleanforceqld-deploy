Our Story

Since 2009, Clean Force QLD has been at the forefront of commercial cleaning services in Australia. What started as a small operation has grown into one of the country's most trusted cleaning companies, serving over 300 businesses nationwide.

Our success can be attributed to providing good old-fashioned, personalized customer service coupled with delivery of a consistent service at competitive market rates. We believe where people work matters, and that's why we get personal to create better workplace environments.

Centred on a promise of Quality, Value & Flexibility, our integrated facilities management is personalized to each client in the public and private sectors across Queensland and Australia.
